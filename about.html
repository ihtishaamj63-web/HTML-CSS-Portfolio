About me

A few things you should know

I’m Ihtishaam Johnson, a full‑stack developer in training with a strong foundation in biodiversity and conservation biology. I love turning complex problems into simple, beautiful, and intuitive digital solutions.

After finishing first in my class at Pelican Park High – where I also excelled in English and History – I went on to earn a BSc in Biodiversity & Conservation Biology from the University of the Western Cape. There, I developed rigorous data analysis skills and became proficient with the Microsoft Office Suite.

Now at Life Choices Academy, I’m channelling that analytical mindset into web development. I’m experienced with Python, HTML, CSS, and I’m currently diving deeper into JavaScript.
